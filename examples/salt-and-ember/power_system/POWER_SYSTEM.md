# Power System

Worked example. Deliberately small: one ability with hard limits, because a small
system with enforceable costs generates more tension than a large one with vague
edges.

Name: Fire-drawing from seawater
Type: CUSTOM
Status: ESTABLISHED

## Core rules
- Fire can be drawn only from seawater, never from fresh water, stored water, or anything on land
- Only at night, without exception
- Only within sight of open water; a roof, a hull, or a cliff breaks it
- The drawn fire behaves like ordinary fire for the rest of that night

## Costs
| Ability | Effect | Cost (immediate) | Cost (delayed) | Recovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Draw | A burning line or sphere of seawater fire | Salt in the mouth; bleeding from the sinuses | Cannot draw again until the next night | One full night |
| Hold | Sustains drawn fire without relighting | Accelerates exhaustion | Two nights of recovery | Two nights |
| Release | Disposes of drawn fire harmlessly | None | Draws attention; the salt smell carries | Immediate |

No capability without a cost. A power with no cost generates no tension, because
nothing is at stake in using it.

## Limitations
Operational, not rhetorical:

- Night only
- Open water in sight only
- Seawater only
- One draw per night without the two-night recovery
- Cannot be done while inside a vessel below deck (not open water in sight)

## Ranks
| Rank | Capability gained | What it does not fix | Ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Apprentice | Draw one line, once per night | Cannot hold; cannot release unseen | Line only |
| Journeyman | Hold for an hour; release unseen | Still one draw per night | Sphere under a fathom |
| Master | Hold for a night; draw twice | Cannot draw fresh water, cannot draw inland | Sphere three fathoms |

Ranks change capability, not magnitude. An apprentice who cannot hold cannot be
traded against a journeyman by raw output, which is why Wenna's rank matters less
than her willingness to spend the recovery.

## Progression
How advancement works: the Guild certifies, and certification requires a master to
vouch. Wenna's certification is offered in Chapter 7 as leverage.
What it costs: an apprenticeship debt that binds for three years
What persists from earlier stages: the night and seawater limits never change. No rank
lets a fire-drawer work by day or inland, so the constraints remain the story's
constant.

## Techniques
- Trawl-line: a drawn line drawn along a hull, cheapest to make, hardest to aim
- Standing salt: a sphere held at one point, exhausting to sustain
- The under-keel draw: worked from below the waterline, effective against a sealed hull
- Release unseen: for when being seen is worse than the cost

## Matchups and counters
| Matchup | Counter | What the counter costs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire-drawer at night | Seal the hull; a sealed vessel is not open water | A full night of work and every fire-drawer in range |
| Fire-drawer inland | Nothing; the ability does not function | — |
| Fire-drawer exhausted | Wait one night | A night in which anything can happen |

The third row is why Wenna's exhaustion is a genuine tactical state rather than a
dramatic convenience.

## Known exploits
Maintained by `power-exploit`.

| Exploit | Conditions | Severity | Canon status | Minimal repair |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Draw through a porthole | An open porthole counts as sight of open water | Moderate | UNRESOLVED | Decide whether a porthole counts; if it does, every ship becomes a risk, which may be the point |
| Rain as open water | Rainwater is fresh, so the seawater rule should exclude it; no one has tested it | Low | UNRESOLVED | Record the ruling |

Both are recorded as unresolved rather than patched, because both could be intended
limitations of the system rather than oversights.

## Unknown mechanics
Deliberately unexplained: why the ability is night-bound. The Guild's own account is
that night is when the sea gives heat back. Whether that is true is U-001 territory
and is left open.
