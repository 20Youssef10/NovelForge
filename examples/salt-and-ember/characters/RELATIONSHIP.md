# Relationship

Worked example. Relationships are two-way and asymmetric; a one-sided record is a
character description rather than a relationship.

- ID: R-001
- Character A: Wenna Ardray
- Character B: Maro Ardray (brother)
- Status: STRAINED

## Asymmetry
What Wenna wants from Maro: that he tell her she was right about the sealing
What Maro wants from Wenna: that she stop spending herself proving she is capable
Where these conflict: she needs him to be the witness to her vindication; he needs
her to accept that being right was never the point

## History
Shared events: the wreck that killed their mother; eleven years of shared
apprenticeships; four years estranged since she joined the Guild
Trust established: he kept her secret about the seal stones
Trust broken: she chose the Guild over him when he asked her not to
Debts (both directions): she owes him four years of presence; he owes her the truth
about the night of the sealing, which he does not have

## Current state
Last significant interaction: Chapter 3, the argument at the rope ladder
Standing with third parties who know of this: the Guild knows only that they are
siblings and that she visits
Volatile factors: the Chapter 6 choice; her master's leverage over her

## Knowledge
What Wenna believes about Maro: that he is stubborn and safe and will not leave
What Maro believes about Wenna: that she would choose the Guild over him again
Where the two accounts diverge: he believes she still does not know what he knows

## Capacity to change
What Maro would have to do for Wenna to change view: say plainly that he was wrong
to ask her not to join, which costs him the only leverage he has
What Wenna would sacrifice to keep it: the certification, which is the only thing
her master cannot take back
Moral boundary each would have to cross: he would have to lie to her; she would have
to admit the sealing might have been right

## Voice
How they speak to each other: neither uses maritime terms; they argue in the clipped
precision that the water taught them

## Arc
Intended trajectory: estranged → forced cooperation in Chapter 5 → a colder, more
honest alliance
Turning points: the Chapter 5 crossing; the Chapter 6 argument
Obligation IDs: RELATIONSHIP_SETUP O-005

## Audit
Does either side exist only to serve the plot: no; both have goals that conflict with
Wenna's independently of Maro's survival
Consequences of the last change acknowledged downstream: her estrangement is why she
is not warned about the seal stones
