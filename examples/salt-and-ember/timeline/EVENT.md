- ID: T-004
- Date/time: three years before Day 1, late autumn
- Location: the northern route, seal stones
- Canon branch: MAIN

## Participants
Present: Ilvar (Wenna's master), guild officers
Absent but affected: Wenna, sixteen, who sees from the stones

## Causal position
Caused by: UNRESOLVED (U-001, who gave the order)
Causes: the sealing of the northern route (C-002); T-001 by establishing Wenna's
knowledge of the master at the stones

## Presentation
Presented in chapter / scene: Chapter 1 scene 1.2 as Maro's recollection; the
present-tense sighting is Chapter 1 scene 1.3
Flashback or out of sequence: the present sighting precedes the backstory in the
text, while the chronology is the reverse. Both orders are recorded deliberately

## Effects
Immediate consequences: the route is sealed; Wenna witnesses it and says nothing
Delayed consequences: O-001; her silence in Chapter 7
Graph edges: T-004 CAUSED_BY unresolved
Faction and world state changes: the Guild's control of the northern route

## Dependencies
Chapters relying on this: 1, 6, 7, 8, 9
Obligations arising: O-002
Mystery information states changed: the reader and Wenna both know something is wrong
and neither knows what

The reversal between chronological and presentational order is intentional and is the
reason both are recorded. Conflating them produces a false continuity error, because a
reader encountering the flashback before the event has no basis to flag it.
