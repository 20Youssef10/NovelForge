## Overloaded windows
Points where more obligations fall due than can be carried.

| Window | Obligations due | Recommended action | Author decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chapter 7 | O-001, O-002, O-007 | Move O-002's reveal to Chapter 8; Chapter 7 should carry the master confrontation, not also the sealing answer | Deferred — author wants to see whether Chapter 7 can hold all three |

This is an open risk, not a settled plan. If Chapter 7 attempts all three, expect a
Major pacing finding and two obligations discharged without weight.

## Never invent payoffs
Do not add a resolution merely to clear a row. A payoff added to satisfy this index
rather than serve the story is worse than the debt it clears. `ABANDONED` with a
recorded reason is a legitimate resolution.
