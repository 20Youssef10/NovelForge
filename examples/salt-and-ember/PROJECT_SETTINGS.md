# Project Settings

Worked example. Shows how configuration decisions are recorded as project settings
rather than buried in a plan.

## Narration
POV model: third limited, one POV character per scene
Default POV character: Wenna (establishing chapters); Maro from Chapter 4
Tense: past
Narrative distance: close third, physical sensation privileged over summary
Multiple POV permitted: yes, but never two POV characters in one scene

## Autonomy
Approval strictness: `strict`

| Level | MEDIUM risk | HIGH risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `strict` | Ask before acting | Ask before acting |
| `standard` | Proceed when clearly local and reversible | Ask before acting |
| `permissive` | Proceed | Ask before acting |

Selected: `strict`

Chosen after Chapter 3, where an unrequested MEDIUM restructure moved a scene
boundary and had to be undone. Chosen before writing resumed.

HIGH-risk work requires approval at every level. No setting waives that.

Autonomy ceiling: do not restructure scenes that have already been authored without
asking, even at `permissive`.

Reason for the current level: an unrequested restructure cost a chapter of work to
reverse.

## Scope conventions
Target chapter length: 3,500–4,500 words
Content boundaries: no on-screen violence against children; the sinking is off-page
Sensitivities the agent should flag rather than write: coercion framed as romantic;
any treatment of drowning as aesthetically attractive

## Style
Style DNA location: `style/style-dna.md`
Language variant: en-GB
Tone the project avoids: archaic diction, swagger, anything that reads as a wiki entry
about its own world
