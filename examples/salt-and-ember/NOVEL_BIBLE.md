# Novel Bible

Worked example. This is a small, complete, fictional project used to demonstrate
what a filled Novel Bible looks like. It is not a novel; it exists so the shape of a
real project is visible rather than described.

## Identity
- Title: Salt and Ember
- Genre: dark fantasy
- Subdomains: coastal smuggling, elemental craft, factional politics
- Languages: en-GB
- POV model: third limited, one POV character per scene
- Tense: past
- Status: DRAFTING
- Canon branch: MAIN

## Premise
- Logline: A salt-ship's apprentice who can pull fire out of seawater has one night to decide which of two collapsing trade routes is worth saving.
- Core conflict: The Guild that trains her wants the northern route sealed. Her brother is aboard a ship on it.
- Themes: loyalty as a form of self-betrayal; what a coast owes the sea that feeds it
- Intended reader experience: a small moral choice made under impossible arithmetic

## Canon policy
Canon states: `PERMANENT_CANON`, `ARC_CANON`, `CHAPTER_CANON`, `SCENE_STATE`, `PROPOSAL`, `UNRESOLVED`, `RECON`.

- Permanent canon: `canon/facts.md`
- Known retcons: `canon/retcons.md`
- Unresolved questions: `canon/unresolved.md`

Deliberately absent: `mysteries/` is present but `factions/` is not, because the
Guild and the Salt Compact are represented through their effects on characters
rather than modelled as institutions yet. `series/` is absent; this is a standalone
novel. Empty directories would be noise.

## System notes
Project files are canonical. Memory accelerates retrieval and never overrides them.
Stable IDs connect the graph, obligations, canon impact, timeline, branches,
research, and reports.

There is no quiet retcon. HIGH-risk changes require author approval before
execution, not after.
