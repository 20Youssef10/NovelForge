## Incomplete migrations
None. R-001 was applied across chapters 4–6, the character sheet, and the
foreshadowing record in one pass.

Had this been left mid-migration, chapters 4 and 5 would still have referred to
Maro as lost, which is exactly the kind of contradiction that surfaces three
chapters later as a mystery nobody can explain.
