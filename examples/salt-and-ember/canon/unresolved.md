# Unresolved Canon

Worked example. Questions the novel has genuinely not decided. Recording them stops
the system inventing an answer and later treating that answer as intentional.

- ID: U-001
- Question: Who gave the order to seal the northern route?
- Why unresolved: author undecided; the reveal is available for a later book or never
- Candidate answers: the Guild's own council; the Salt Compact, as leverage; Wenna's
  master, acting alone
- Which is canon now: none
- Affected areas: mystery M-001, chapters 7–9, the ending
- Deadline or reveal target: UNRESOLVED
- Blocks what: nothing currently; the ending can be written without it
- Resolved on / by: —

- ID: U-002
- Question: Does Wenna's fire-drawing leave her body changed in some permanent way?
- Why unresolved: deliberately open; the author wants the ambiguity preserved
- Candidate answers: a scar; a shortened lifespan; nothing observable
- Which is canon now: none
- Affected areas: power system limits, foreshadow F-004, relationship R-001
- Deadline or reveal target: UNRESOLVED
- Blocks what: the Chapter 9 ending
- Resolved on / by: —

## Collision warning
U-001 and U-002 cannot both resolve favourably for Wenna without collapsing the moral
tension of Chapter 6. Any resolution pair must be checked against the Chapter 6
choice before either is written.
