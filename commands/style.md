description: Analyse, drift-check, or compare the novel's Style DNA
argument-hint: "<analyse|drift|compare> [scope]"
---

Work the novel's voice.

1. Load `narrative-style`, and `style-drift` for `drift`.
2. For `analyse`, derive Style DNA from passages the author confirms as characteristic, and confirm the baseline with them.
3. For `drift`, compare against that DNA rather than a generic genre standard, and separate intentional evolution from accidental drift.
4. For `compare`, put passages side by side against the recorded properties.
5. Never flatten distinctive voice to achieve consistency. Report drift and let the author decide.
6. Record findings in `style/STYLE_AUDIT.md`.
