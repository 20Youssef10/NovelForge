Work a power system.

1. Load `power-system`, and `power-exploit` for `exploit`.
2. For `check`, verify rules, costs, limits, and ranks are recorded and that the text respects them.
3. For `exploit`, run the taxonomy in `references/SCALING_AND_EXPLOITS.md`. Record conditions, severity, canon status, and the narrowest repair.
4. For `scale`, explain any unexpected outcome from the scaling dimensions, and confirm the reason is visible in the scene.
5. Never invent an ability or exception to solve a plot problem. Record it as a proposal and route to `canon-impact`.
6. An exception that can bypass any rule makes every rule optional. Flag it if found.
