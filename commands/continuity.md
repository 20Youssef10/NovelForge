Check continuity.

1. Load `continuity`, and `timeline` if chronology is involved.
2. Check by consequence: knowledge states first, then capability and physical state, then terminology.
3. For each conflict, cite both locations and classify it: likely error, intentional change, unresolved ambiguity, or retcon candidate.
4. Cross-check every significant decision against `knowledge/LEDGER.md`.
5. Repair LOW-risk errors automatically. Route anything classified as a retcon to `canon-impact`.
6. Never rewrite canon silently.
