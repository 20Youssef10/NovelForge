Plan before drafting.

1. Load `novel-planner`, and `context-manager` if the work is multi-file.
2. Match granularity to the project. Do not force every novel into identical levels.
3. Produce a proposal including purpose, causality, conflict, stakes, character movement, information movement, pacing, thematic function, expected consequences, dependencies, and unresolved decisions.
4. Register every promise, setup, and dependency this plan creates, at the moment of creating it.
5. Identify the obvious reader expectation and develop alternatives that remain causally earned.
6. Stop for author approval before any drafting. Do not proceed on your own judgement.
