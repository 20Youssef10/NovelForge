---
description: Create, compare, merge, or roll back a story branch
argument-hint: "<create|compare|merge|rollback> [name]"
---

Work with branches.

1. Load `version-control`. Snapshot first; a change without a parent cannot be evaluated or undone.
2. For `create`, record parent, purpose, scope, and isolation. A branch that edits main canon is not a branch.
3. For `compare`, diff canon, timeline, graph, obligations, and character state against the parent.
4. For `merge`, run canon impact, continuity, timeline reconciliation of both chronologies, graph, obligation, and quality checks. Resolve conflicts one at a time, recording each decision. Never merge silently.
5. For `rollback`, record that the rollback happened. Undoing an approved decision silently is its own defect.
6. After a merge, invalidate branch-scoped memory and re-run the gate on main.
