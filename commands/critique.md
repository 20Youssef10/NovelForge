Independent critique.

1. Load `critique`. Adopt an independent stance; the goal is accuracy, not reassurance.
2. For a full review, read `references/CHECK_CATALOGUE.md`.
3. Check `quality/FINDINGS.md` first. Do not re-report an open finding as new, and do not re-raise an accepted risk without cause.
4. Cite a location for every finding. A finding without evidence is an opinion.
5. Classify severity by consequence, not by effort.
6. Log new findings with stable IDs. Move to Resolved only once a fix is verified, not attempted.
7. Report rather than rewrite, except verified LOW-risk local repairs.
