Work a mystery.

1. Load `mystery-engine`, plus `reader-knowledge` for what the reader plausibly holds.
2. For `analyse`, run the solvability test from `references/FAIRNESS.md`: assemble the evidence available at the reveal and check a careful reader could have reached the conclusion.
3. For `reveal`, verify reveal prerequisites are planted and that post-reveal consequences are planned for every character.
4. Keep author truth, character knowledge, reader knowledge, and evidence visibility separate.
5. Record in `mysteries/MYSTERY.md`, and register the reveal as an obligation.
6. Report fairness failures; do not fix them silently by planting clues in the same pass.
