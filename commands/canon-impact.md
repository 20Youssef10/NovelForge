Analyse a canon change before applying it.

1. Load `canon-manager` and read `references/CHANGE_PROCEDURE.md`.
2. Fill `templates/CHANGE_IMPACT.md` completely before proposing any edit.
3. Trace every dependency: graph, obligations, plans, character sheets, timeline, mysteries, foreshadowing, factions, power rules, style, and branch state.
4. Classify risk. A change that cannot be reversed cleanly is HIGH regardless of size.
5. Snapshot via `version-control` before applying.
6. Present options with their gains and costs. For HIGH risk, get approval before execution, not after.
7. Record the change, the reason, the approver, and the migration status. A retcon is not complete until migration is COMPLETE.
