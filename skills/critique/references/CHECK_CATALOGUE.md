# Critique Check Catalogue

Detailed inventory for `critique`. Load when conducting a full review and the specific checks need enumerating. The skill body carries the method and severity scale; this file carries the catalogue.

## Severity, applied consistently

Severity describes **consequence**, not effort. A one-word change that invalidates an arc is Critical.

| Severity | Meaning |
|---| --- |
| **Critical** | Breaks the story: unearned ending, collapsed mystery, a core rule the text ignores, a protagonist whose arc does not happen |
| **Major** | Materially damages the narrative: unmotivated turn, forgotten payoff, sustained voice collapse, a rule broken with no acknowledgement |
| **Moderate** | Weakens specific scenes: pacing stall, thin transition, local style drift, a scene that could be cut |
| **Minor** | Polish: word choice, repetition, formatting, register slips |

## Check catalogue

### Structure
- Does the unit change something, or could it be deleted without loss?
- Are scenes multi-function, or is one function stretched across several?
- Do turns follow from conditions, or do they arrive?
- Is escalation raising stakes and reducing options, or only increasing volume?
- Does the ending resolve through choice, or through explanation?

### Causality
- Each major turn: is there a prior condition, a trigger, a decision, and a mechanism?
- Any outcome with no route by which it could have happened?
- Coincidence used as repair rather than as design?
- Any reversal that exists because the scene needs a turn?

### Consequence
- Do downstream effects of significant actions appear?
- Has the story silently returned to a prior state?
- Is recovery faster than the world's own logic permits?
- Do institutions react more calmly than their motives allow?

### Character
- Do decisions follow from goals, beliefs, and knowledge held?
- Is any action taken on information never acquired?
- Are moral boundaries crossed without anything breaking them?
- Is transformation earned through choices, or delivered by exposition?
- Is any character present only to deliver information or enable a beat?
- Do relationships show asymmetry, or are they symmetrical summaries?

### Information control
- Could a careful reader have reached each reveal?
- Is any clue meaningful only in hindsight, revealed as though planted?
- Has the text disclosed more than it intended?
- Does any character know what the narration has not established they could know?

### Mystery and payoff
- Setups live, paid, or recorded as deliberately abandoned?
- Payoff windows honoured, or is a window silently overstayed?
- Overloaded resolutions: several payoffs in one chapter, each landing without weight?
- Red herrings resolvable rather than merely dropped?

### Continuity
- Contradictions with two cited locations each?
- Knowledge-state violations?
- Ages, injuries, and possessions consistent with their last appearance?
- Terminology consistent with the glossary?

### Style and voice
- Consistent with the project's Style DNA?
- Deviations correlated with a narrative development, or isolated?
- Head-hopping, omniscient leakage, or distance violations?
- Does each character's speech remain distinguishable?

### Originality
- Generic prose by the swap test or the deletion test?
- Clichés classified as cliché versus intentional convention?
- Trope expectations created, then paid or deliberately deferred?
- Motifs recurring with evolution, or decorative repetition?

### Theme
- Theme expressed through choices, or stated in narration?
- Counterexamples present, or decided before the story began?
- Orphaned resolution answering a question never raised?

### Pacing and tension
- Stalls, repetition, over-compression, setup without movement?
- Stakes real, or announced?
- Fake tension where threatened danger never arrives?
- Breathing space, or escalation fatigue?

### Power, battle, and factions
- Rules respected, costs paid, exceptions recorded?
- Reversals caused rather than granted?
- Factions responding with partial information and internal disagreement?
- Rank victories explained from the scaling dimensions?

### Research
- Load-bearing external claims verified or marked uncertain?
- Sources dated, with stale claims flagged?
- Creative invention misrecorded as external fact?

## Method reminders

- Every finding cites a location. A finding without evidence is an opinion.
- Distinguish what the text does from what you infer it intends.
- Report rather than rewrite, except for verified LOW-risk local repairs.
- Independent review means finding what is wrong, not reassuring — and equally, not manufacturing criticism. A finding you cannot evidence is a liability.
