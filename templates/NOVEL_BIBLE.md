## Canon policy
Canon states: `PERMANENT_CANON`, `ARC_CANON`, `CHAPTER_CANON`, `SCENE_STATE`, `PROPOSAL`, `UNRESOLVED`, `RECON`.

- Permanent canon: `canon/facts.md`
- Known retcons: `canon/retcons.md`
- Unresolved questions: `canon/unresolved.md`

There is no quiet retcon. A change made in a branch does not alter main canon until an explicit, gated merge. Research findings do not become canon by being true.

## Risk and approval
- `LOW` — grammar, repetition, formatting, metadata, local continuity repairs. May execute automatically.
- `MEDIUM` — bounded restructuring, limited behavioural change. Confirm scope.
- `HIGH` — canon or retcon, major death, ending change, major relationship restructuring, global timeline, power-system rules, major faction state, cross-arc dependency, research promoted to canon, branch merge, cross-novel memory transfer, large-scale style change. **Requires author approval before execution.**
