## Rules
- Snapshot before any meaningful edit. A change without a recorded parent cannot be evaluated, and a change that turns out wrong cannot be undone without guessing.
- Match scope to the change. A scene repair needs a scoped snapshot; a restructure needs a full one. Snapshotting everything makes history unreadable.
- Never erase history to make a change look clean.
