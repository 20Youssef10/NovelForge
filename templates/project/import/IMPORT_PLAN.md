## Ambiguities preserved
Things deliberately not resolved: names that may be one person or two, setups that may be forgotten or abandoned, dates that cannot be reconciled. Each stays `UNRESOLVED` with its possibilities recorded.

## Not done
Anything deliberately skipped, and why. Intake that silently omits a domain is worse than intake that declares the omission.
