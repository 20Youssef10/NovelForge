## Rules

- Every finding gets a stable ID at first report. Never re-report an open finding as new.
- A finding moves to Resolved only when verified fixed, not when a rewrite was attempted.
- Accepted risks require a reason and a named author. "Not planning to fix it" is a reason; silence is not.
- Do not delete entries. A removed finding and a fixed finding are indistinguishable, and only one of them is true.
