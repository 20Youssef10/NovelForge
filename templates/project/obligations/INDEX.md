# Narrative Obligation Index

What the story owes its reader. Check this before planning any chapter, and at every quality gate.

Types: `PROMISE` · `FORESHADOW` · `MYSTERY` · `CHARACTER_SETUP` · `RELATIONSHIP_SETUP` · `CONSEQUENCE` · `WORLD_SETUP` · `POWER_SETUP`

Status: `OPEN` · `FULFILLED` · `TRANSFORMED` · `ABANDONED` · `BLOCKED`

## Overloaded windows
Points where more obligations fall due than can be carried. Resolving several in one chapter gives the reader several moments of relief and no shape; choose which to move, and record the deferral so it is a decision rather than an oversight.

## Never invent payoffs
Do not add a resolution merely to clear a row. A payoff added to satisfy this index rather than serve the story is worse than the debt it clears. `ABANDONED` with a recorded reason is a legitimate state.
