## Rules
- Acquisition point is mandatory. Knowledge without a stated origin is the most frequent serious continuity error.
- Faction and institutional knowledge is tracked per informed individual, then aggregated — see `factions/FACTION.md`.
- Do not resolve a contradiction by deleting a row. Record the correction.
