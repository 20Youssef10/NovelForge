## System notes
Canon states: PERMANENT_CANON, ARC_CANON, CHAPTER_CANON, SCENE_STATE, PROPOSAL, UNRESOLVED, RETCON.
Risk levels: LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH — HIGH requires author approval before execution, not after.
Approval gates, branch isolation, and multi-novel isolation are absolute. There is no such thing as a quiet retcon, and no cross-novel retrieval on similarity alone.
